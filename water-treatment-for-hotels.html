Water Treatment for Hotels, Resorts & Restaurants

Hotels use water everywhere — guest rooms, kitchens, laundry, boilers and pools. Hard water leaves scale in geysers, boilers, dishwashers and laundry machines, and makes linen stiff and dull. Guests also notice white marks on taps and glassware.

A correctly sized water softener on the main supply, an RO plant for drinking and kitchen water, and UV disinfection solve most of these issues. Larger properties also need an STP for wastewater.

Frequently asked questions

What size softener does a hotel need?

It depends on daily water use and hardness. As a starting point use our softener calculator, then share the result for an exact model.

Should a hotel use RO for all water?

Usually not. A softener treats bulk water for bathrooms, laundry and boilers; RO is used only for drinking and kitchen water.

Do you supply salt and spares for softeners?

Softener spares such as multiport valves, resin vessels and brine accessories are available; ask on WhatsApp for current stock.
