Water Treatment for Hospitals, Clinics & Laboratories

Hospitals need several grades of water: safe drinking water for patients and staff, soft water for CSSD, laundry and boilers, and low-conductivity water for laboratory and some equipment. Hospitals also generate wastewater that needs proper treatment.

We supply RO and DM plants, softeners, UV systems, conductivity and TDS instruments, and STP/ETP solutions, along with spares and chemicals for regular maintenance.
