How to choose

Decide first whether you need only a reading or also automatic control. An indicator shows the value; a controller has a relay and can act on it. Match the sensor to the temperature and the water condition.

Compatibility: Sensors are field replaceable. Relay versions can drive a dosing pump or diverter valve. 4-20mA and RS485 options feed panels and dataloggers.
