Standard or jumbo?

Jumbo (big-bore) cartridges have a larger diameter, so they hold more dirt and allow more flow. They need a jumbo housing. A standard cartridge will not seal in a jumbo housing, and vice versa — check your housing first. See the cartridge housing size guide.

How many cartridges does my plant need?

As a planning guide, allow about 500–700 LPH per 20 inch standard cartridge on RO duty. A 2,000 LPH plant therefore uses a multi-cartridge housing with around 3–4 × 20" cartridges, or fewer jumbo cartridges.

When to change

  • When the pressure difference across the housing reaches about 0.7–1 kg/cm²
  • Or every 30–45 days on clean water, sooner on dirty borewell water
  • Never wash and reuse a 5 micron cartridge before an RO — it lets particles through

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Gopani ClaryWound and RO Protect?

ClaryWound is a general 5 micron wound cartridge in 10 to 40 inch lengths. RO Protect Ultra is a 20 inch 5 micron cartridge made as the final guard before an RO pump.

Can a jumbo cartridge fit a standard housing?

No. Jumbo cartridges need a jumbo housing, and a standard cartridge will not seal in a jumbo housing.

How often should Gopani cartridges be changed?

When the pressure drop across the housing reaches about 0.7 to 1 kg/cm², or roughly every 30 to 45 days on clean water.

Which micron cartridge should be used before RO?

A 5 micron cartridge is standard before the RO high pressure pump.

Can cartridges be washed and reused?

Not before an RO plant. A washed cartridge lets particles through and damages the membrane.
