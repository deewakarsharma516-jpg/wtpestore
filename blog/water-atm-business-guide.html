Water ATM machine — card, coin and QR types, setup and price

Aqua Filtration System, Faridabad · 9+ years in water treatment · Updated October 2026

Short answer: A water ATM is a self-service RO water dispenser. The customer pays by RFID card, coin or QR (UPI), and the machine releases a measured quantity through a flow sensor and solenoid valve. It sits on top of an RO plant and storage tank, and is widely used by panchayats, municipal bodies, CSR projects and private operators.

How it works

  1. RO water is stored in a tank, usually with UV on the outlet line
  2. The customer taps a card, inserts coins or scans a QR code
  3. The controller opens a solenoid valve; a flow sensor counts the litres
  4. When the paid quantity is reached, the valve closes and the balance or sale is recorded

What else you need

  • RO plant sized for peak sales — typically 250 to 1,000 LPH for one or two taps
  • Storage tank and a UV purifier on the dispensing line
  • Shelter / kiosk, power supply and drainage for RO reject
  • Remote monitoring (optional) — see daily litres and income from your phone
  • Water testing to confirm the treated water quality

Setup checklist

  1. Test the raw water (TDS, hardness, iron) — see what to test
  2. Estimate daily sales in litres and peak hour demand
  3. Choose the RO capacity and tank size
  4. Choose the payment type and number of taps
  5. Plan reject water disposal or reuse
  6. Take local permissions where required

Frequently asked questions

How does a water ATM work?

The customer pays by card, coin or QR code, and the controller opens a solenoid valve while a flow sensor measures the litres dispensed.

Which water ATM type is best?

Card-only suits fixed users like villages or factories. Card plus QR or coin suits public locations with walk-in customers.

What RO capacity is needed for a water ATM?

Usually 250 to 1,000 LPH for one or two taps, depending on daily sales and peak demand.

Can I add more taps later?

Yes. Each additional tap needs one more flow sensor and solenoid valve.

Is UV needed in a water ATM?

It is recommended on the dispensing line so stored RO water stays free of bacteria.
