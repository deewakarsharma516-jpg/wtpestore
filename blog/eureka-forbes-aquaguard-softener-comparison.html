Which one for you

  • One bathroom / shower: I-250 or I-600 (manual, no electricity)
  • Whole flat or small house: C-500, I-1200 or C-1400 (automatic, time or flow based)
  • Villa, large house, small hotel: I-2500 or C-3000
  • Building, commercial, guest house: AWS 5000 or 7500
Quick check: daily litres × hardness ÷ 500 tells you how many "500 ppm litres" you use. Pick a model whose output covers at least 2–3 days, so it does not regenerate every day.

Manual or automatic?

Manual models need you to add salt and run the regeneration yourself; they need no power and cost less. Automatic models regenerate on their own by time or by litres used, which suits families who do not want to track it. See how regeneration works.

Frequently asked questions

Which Aquaguard softener is best for a bathroom?

The manual AWS I-250 or I-600 models, which need no electricity and suit one bathroom or shower line.

Which Aquaguard softener suits a whole house?

Automatic models such as the C-500, I-1200 or C-1400 for flats and small houses, and the I-2500 or C-3000 for larger homes.

What is output between regeneration?

The litres of soft water a softener gives before it needs salt regeneration, stated by the maker at 500 ppm hardness. At lower hardness it is higher.
