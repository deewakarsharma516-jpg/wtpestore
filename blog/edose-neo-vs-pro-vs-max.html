Edose NEO vs NEO PRO vs MAX — which model for which plant

Aqua Filtration System, Faridabad · 9+ years in water treatment · Updated September 2026

Short answer: NEO covers most RO plants. NEO PRO includes a level switch and is better where the tank cannot be watched daily. HP is for line pressures up to 10 kg/cm². MAX is the motorised series for large industrial duties above 25 LPH.

Basic Edose vs NEO — only ₹600 apart

The Basic Edose 6 LPH is ₹5,460 and the NEO 6 LPH is ₹6,090 — a difference of only ₹630. The NEO is better built and lasts longer.

Our advice: for that small a difference, take the NEO. Choose Basic only if the budget is genuinely very tight.

NEO vs NEO PRO — what you actually get

The main difference is the level switch, which is included on the NEO PRO. Its job is to stop the pump automatically when the tank empties.

NEUTRA and CHLORO — automatic dosing

These models include a sensor, so the pump adjusts its own dose. No manual setting is required.

  • NEUTRA/CHLORO with pH/ORP sensor — ₹25,725, doses automatically on pH or chlorine
  • CHLORO with FRC sensor — ₹70,350, doses on measured free residual chlorine
  • DUAL 10 LPH — ₹51,450, two chemicals together

These are used for pH correction in ETP and for chlorine control in pools and water treatment, where the dose has to change constantly.

Optional extras

  • Pulse input (₹1,680) — dose in proportion to a flow meter signal, so chemical follows water
  • 4-20mA input (₹1,680) — dose on a signal from a sensor or panel
  • Relay output with level switch (₹2,625) — alarm or panel signal when the tank empties

Pulse input is particularly useful — dosing then rises and falls automatically with plant flow. On plants where flow varies, it is well worth having.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Edose NEO and NEO PRO?

The NEO PRO includes a level switch and has a heavier, more industrial build. On a NEO the level switch is fitted separately for around a thousand rupees. If you only want the switch, a NEO plus a separate switch works out considerably cheaper.

Which Edose model is most commonly used?

The NEO 6 LPH and NEO 10 LPH, because the dosing requirement of most RO plants falls within that range and the price is reasonable.

When is the MAX series needed?

When dosing exceeds twenty five LPH, such as in ETP, STP or large industrial plants. These are motorised and built for heavy continuous duty, which small solenoid pumps are not designed for.

What does pulse input do?

It lets the dosing pump run on a signal from a flow meter, so the chemical dose rises and falls with the water flow. On plants where flow varies this is very useful, because the setting does not have to be changed manually.

What are the NEUTRA and CHLORO models for?

They include a sensor that continuously measures pH or chlorine, and the pump adjusts its own dose accordingly. They are used for pH correction in ETP and for chlorine control in swimming pools and water treatment, where the required dose changes constantly.
