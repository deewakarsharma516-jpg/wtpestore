When a bag filter is better than a cartridge

Aqua Filtration System, Faridabad · 9+ years in water treatment · Updated September 2026

Short answer: A bag filter is the better choice when the water is very dirty and cartridges are choking weekly. A bag holds several times more dirt than a cartridge, so it lasts much longer and reduces the work of changing.

But bag filters are usually available in coarser ratings (25 to 100 micron), so ahead of an RO plant a bag is not sufficient on its own — a cartridge must follow it.

When to fit a bag filter

  • Cartridges choking weekly — the clearest indicator
  • River, pond or monsoon water
  • Filter feed in an ETP or STP
  • Cooling tower side stream
  • Pre-filtration at higher flows

When a cartridge is still better

  • The final stage ahead of RO, where 5 micron is needed and bags are rarely that fine
  • Small plants on clean water
  • Where flow is low

The best arrangement — both together

On larger plants or dirty water we normally fit:

  1. Screen filter — coarse particles, cleaned and reused (Y-type screen)
  2. Bag filter — 25 to 50 micron, holding the bulk of the dirt
  3. Cartridge 5 micron — final protection, and now it lasts much longer
What this achieves: the more expensive 5 micron cartridge now lasts two to three months instead of a week. Both cost and effort come down, and the membrane is properly protected.

The advantage of a swing bolt

The SS304 swing bolt housing is expensive at ₹35,175, but changing a bag is very quick — turn the bolts and the lid opens, with no spanner required.

When that is worth paying for: where the bag has to be changed daily or every other day on very dirty water. Opening with a spanner each time takes time and wears the threads.

How to change a bag

  1. Stop the pump and release pressure
  2. Open the lid (swing bolt or nuts)
  3. Lift the bag out by the top in one movement, or the dirt falls back in
  4. Clean the basket
  5. Fit the new bag, ensuring its ring seats correctly on the housing seat
  6. Close the lid, vent air and check for leaks
If the bag ring is not seated correctly, water bypasses the bag and passes unfiltered. Everything looks normal from outside while no filtration is taking place at all. Always check the ring when fitting a bag.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a bag filter and a cartridge?

A bag filter holds several times more dirt than a cartridge so it lasts much longer. However its micron rating is usually coarser, so ahead of an RO plant a cartridge still has to follow it.

Is a bag filter alone enough for RO?

No. Bag filters are typically twenty five to one hundred micron whereas RO needs five micron. So a cartridge must be fitted after the bag, otherwise fine particles will reach the membrane.

When should a bag filter be fitted?

When cartridges are choking weekly, or where the water comes from a river, pond or is monsoon affected. Fitting a bag filter typically extends cartridge life to two or three months and reduces overall cost considerably.

Why is a swing bolt housing expensive, and when is it justified?

Its bolts turn to open the lid without a spanner. That is worth paying for where the bag has to be changed daily or every other day, since repeated spanner work takes time and wears the threads.

What should I watch when fitting a bag?

The ring of the bag must seat correctly on the housing seat. If it does not, water bypasses the bag unfiltered while everything looks normal from outside, so this fault goes unnoticed. Always check the ring.
