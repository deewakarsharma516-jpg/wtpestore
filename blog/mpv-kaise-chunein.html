How to select a multiport valve — filter or softener, top or side

Aqua Filtration System, Faridabad · 9+ years in water treatment · Updated September 2026

Short answer: Four things decide the valve — whether it is for a filter or a softener, whether the vessel is top or side mount, the line size in NB, and whether you want manual or automatic. Get those four right and the model follows directly.

The most common mistake is ordering a filter valve for a softener. They look similar but a softener valve has an extra brine port, without which regeneration is impossible.

Filter valve or softener valve

This is the first decision and it cannot be changed later.

Filter valveSoftener valve
PositionsService, backwash, rinse, drainThe same plus brine draw and refill
Brine portNoYes
Used forSand, carbon, multigrade filtersSofteners only
PriceLowerHigher
A softener will not work with a filter valve. Without a brine port the resin cannot be regenerated with salt, so the softener works only until the resin is exhausted and then stops removing hardness altogether.

Top mount or side mount

This must match your vessel — the two are not interchangeable. See our top vs side mount guide.

  • TM in the model name means top mounted
  • SM means side mounted

Additional items for a softener

  • Brine director 15NB — ₹1,312, or 20NB at ₹1,522. This sits in the brine tank and controls salt draw
  • Brine switch for automatic MPV — ₹761, for automatic valves
  • Vacuum breaker 25NB — ₹210, protects the vessel
  • Adaptor 2.5 x 4 — ₹210, where the valve and vessel thread sizes differ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a filter valve and a softener valve?

A softener valve has an additional brine port for drawing salt solution during regeneration, which a filter valve does not have. A softener cannot be regenerated with a filter valve, so it stops removing hardness once the resin is exhausted.

How do I choose the multiport valve size?

Match it to your flow and vessel size. Twenty NB suits vessels up to 1054 and flows to a thousand LPH, twenty five NB to about two thousand LPH, forty NB to five thousand, and larger sizes above that.

Can a top mount valve be used on a side mount vessel?

No, they are not interchangeable. The mounting must be decided along with the vessel, because the internal piping arrangement differs between the two.

What is the difference between unions and flanges on the 3565?

Unions are threaded connections and cost less, while flanges are bolted and suit higher pressure or larger installations. The flanged version is considerably more expensive, so choose it only where the installation genuinely requires it.

What else is needed with a softener valve?

A brine director in the brine tank, which controls salt draw, and for automatic valves a brine switch. A vacuum breaker should also be fitted to protect the vessel.
