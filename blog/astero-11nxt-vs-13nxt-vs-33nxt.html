What BWS means

BWS stands for Backwash. This version also sequences the backwash of a filter or softener, so the pre-treatment is automated along with the RO:

  • 33NXT BWS — ₹24,675
  • 33NXT 7.5HP BWS — ₹28,350
  • 33NXT 10HP BWS — ₹31,500

Note that the BWS cut-out size is larger (305Hx225V), so allow space in the board.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 11NXT, 13NXT and 33NXT?

Only the phase of the pumps. On the 11NXT both pumps are single phase, on the 13NXT the raw water pump is single and the high pressure pump three phase, and on the 33NXT both are three phase. The remaining features are broadly the same.

How do I know which model I need?

Read the nameplate on both pumps, which states the phase and HP. Note the phase of the raw water pump and the high pressure pump, and the model follows directly from that.

What is the difference between the 5HP, 7.5HP and 10HP versions of the 33NXT?

They indicate the maximum capacity of the high pressure pump. The price difference between them is small, so if there is any chance of upsizing the pump later, taking the larger version is sensible.

What is the BWS version for?

BWS stands for backwash. This version also sequences the backwash of a filter or softener automatically, so a separate panel for pre-treatment is not needed. Its cut-out size is larger than the standard version.

When is the 333NXT needed?

When the RO plant has three pumps rather than two, such as a raw water pump, a high pressure pump and a transfer or circulation pump. This model is designed to run all three from one panel.
