UV water purifier — when you need it and how to size it by LPH

Aqua Filtration System, Faridabad · 9+ years in water treatment · Updated October 2026

Short answer: A UV purifier passes water past an ultraviolet lamp that disables bacteria and viruses without adding chemicals. Choose it by your maximum flow in LPH, install it after filtration (on clear water), and change the lamp roughly once a year.

When you need UV

  • After an RO plant, before a storage tank or dispenser — to keep the treated water safe
  • On borewell or tanker water that is otherwise clear but may carry bacteria
  • In hotels, hospitals, schools, offices and water ATMs
  • Food and beverage process water

UV does not remove TDS, hardness, chlorine or dirt. It only disinfects.

Sizing by flow

Always size on the peak flow passing through the unit, not the daily litres. Water moving faster than the rated flow gets less UV exposure.

Installation rules

  • Install on clear water — after the sand filter, cartridge or RO. Turbid water shields bacteria from the light.
  • Mount the chamber so it stays full of water; trapped air reduces effect.
  • Give it a steady power supply; a stabiliser helps where voltage fluctuates.
  • Leave space at one end to pull out the lamp and quartz sleeve for service.

Maintenance

  • Lamp: replace about every 9,000 hours (around one year of continuous running), even if it still glows — the UV output falls before the visible light does.
  • Quartz sleeve: clean every 3–6 months; hard water leaves a coating that blocks UV.

Frequently asked questions

Does a UV purifier reduce TDS?

No. UV only disables bacteria and viruses. TDS needs an RO plant.

How do I choose the UV size?

Choose by the maximum flow in LPH passing through it, not the daily litres. If the pump gives 1,500 LPH, choose a unit rated above that, such as 2,100 LPH.

Where should the UV be installed?

On clear water — after filtration or after the RO — so particles do not shield bacteria from the light.

How often should the UV lamp be changed?

About every 9,000 running hours, roughly once a year on continuous use, even if it still glows.

Is UV needed after RO?

It is recommended where treated water is stored in a tank or dispensed, because bacteria can grow after the RO.
