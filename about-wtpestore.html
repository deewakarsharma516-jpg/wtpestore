About WTPESTORE — Frequently Asked Questions

WTPESTORE is the online store of Aqua Filtration System, Faridabad — water treatment plants, spares and chemicals since 2017.

What is WTPESTORE?

WTPESTORE (www.wtpestore.co.in, also written WTPeSTORE or WTP eStore) is the online water treatment products store of Aqua Filtration System, Faridabad, Haryana, India. It sells RO plants, water softeners, DM/UF/ETP/STP plants, RO membranes, dosing pumps, FRP vessels, flow meters, filters, chemicals and spares with live prices and GST invoice.
